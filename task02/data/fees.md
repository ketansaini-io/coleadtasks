# Aurora Institute of Technology — Fee Structure 2026

All fees are listed in Indian Rupees (INR) per academic year unless stated otherwise.

## B.Tech Tuition Fees (per year)

- **Computer Science and Engineering (CSE):** INR 1,80,000 per year.
- **Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (AI-DS):** INR 1,95,000 per year.
- **Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE):** INR 1,60,000 per year.
- **Mechanical Engineering (ME):** INR 1,40,000 per year.

## M.Tech Tuition Fees (per year)

- **M.Tech Data Science:** INR 1,20,000 per year.
- **M.Tech VLSI Design:** INR 1,10,000 per year.

## Other Mandatory Fees (per year)

- One-time admission fee (first year only): INR 25,000.
- Examination fee: INR 6,000 per year.
- Library and laboratory fee: INR 12,000 per year.
- Student activity and clubs fee: INR 3,000 per year.

## Hostel and Mess (optional)

- Hostel room (shared, double occupancy): INR 60,000 per year.
- Hostel room (single occupancy): INR 90,000 per year.
- Mess charges: INR 48,000 per year.

## Scholarships

- **Merit Scholarship:** Students in the top 5 percent of their program by CGPA receive a
  50 percent tuition waiver for the next academic year.
- **Need-Based Grant:** Students with a family income below INR 4,00,000 per year may apply for
  up to a 40 percent tuition waiver.
- **Sports Scholarship:** State and national level players receive up to INR 50,000 per year.

## Payment Schedule

Tuition fees are paid in two equal installments: the first before the start of the odd semester
(by 15 July) and the second before the start of the even semester (by 15 December). A late
payment incurs a fine of INR 200 per day up to a maximum of INR 5,000. Fees may be paid online
through the AIT student portal or by demand draft payable to "Aurora Institute of Technology".
