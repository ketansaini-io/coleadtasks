# Aurora Institute of Technology — Courses & Programs

## Undergraduate Programs (B.Tech, 4 years)

Aurora Institute of Technology (AIT) offers the following four-year Bachelor of Technology
(B.Tech) programs. Each program requires 160 credits to graduate.

- **B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)** — Intake 120 seats. Core areas:
  data structures, algorithms, operating systems, databases, machine learning and distributed systems.
- **B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (AI-DS)** — Intake 60 seats. Core areas:
  statistics, machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing and big-data systems.
- **B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)** — Intake 90 seats. Core areas:
  signals, VLSI design, embedded systems, communication networks and the Internet of Things.
- **B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering (ME)** — Intake 60 seats. Core areas: thermodynamics,
  manufacturing, robotics and computer-aided design.

## Postgraduate Programs (M.Tech, 2 years)

- **M.Tech in Data Science** — Intake 25 seats. Requires a GATE score or an AIT entrance test.
- **M.Tech in VLSI Design** — Intake 20 seats.

## Eligibility

Admission to B.Tech programs requires a pass in Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry and
Mathematics, and a valid score in the Aurora Joint Entrance Examination (AJEE). The minimum
aggregate required in Class 12 is 60 percent (55 percent for reserved categories).

## Academic Structure

Every B.Tech program runs over 8 semesters. A semester is roughly 16 weeks of instruction
followed by end-semester examinations. Students must maintain a minimum CGPA of 5.0 to remain
in good academic standing. The AI-DS program includes a mandatory 6-month industry internship
in the final year.

## Credits and Electives

Students choose professional electives from the 5th semester onward. A student may take up to
two open electives from a different department. Honours degrees are awarded to students who
complete 20 additional credits of advanced electives with a CGPA of 8.5 or above.
