make-up classes. There are no regular classes on Sunday.

## Academic Calendar 2026–27

- **Odd Semester** begins on 21 July 2026 and ends on 14 November 2026.
- **Odd Semester end examinations:** 17 November to 1 December 2026.
- **Winter break:** 2 December to 31 December 2026.
- **Even Semester** begins on 5 January 2027 and ends on 1 May 2027.
- **Even Semester end examinations:** 4 May to 18 May 2027.
- **Summer break:** 19 May to 15 July 2027.

## Important Dates

- Club Fair: first two weeks of the odd semester.
- Spectra (techno-cultural fest): February 2027.
- Mid-semester tests: roughly week 8 of each semester.
- Last date to register for electives: within the first 10 days of each semester.

## Attendance Windows

Attendance is recorded every period. A student must attend at least 75 percent of the classes
in each course to be eligible to sit for the end-semester examination of that course.
