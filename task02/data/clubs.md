# Aurora Institute of Technology — Student Clubs & Societies

AIT hosts a vibrant student community with technical, cultural and sports clubs. Every student
pays an annual student activity fee that funds these clubs. Club membership is free for enrolled
students.

## Technical Clubs

- **Aurora Coding Club (ACC):** Focuses on competitive programming, open-source contributions and
  weekly algorithm practice. Meets every Wednesday at 5:30 PM in Lab C-204. Hosts the annual
  "CodeStorm" hackathon in March.
- **AI & Robotics Society (AIRS):** Works on machine learning projects, autonomous robots and
  drones. Meets Fridays at 4:00 PM in the Robotics Lab. Runs study groups on deep learning and
  computer vision.
- **Cybersecurity Cell (CySec):** Capture-the-flag competitions, ethical hacking workshops and
  security audits. Meets alternate Saturdays.

## Cultural Clubs

- **Rhythm (Music Club):** Vocal and instrumental music, organizes the "Euphony" cultural night.
- **Expressions (Drama Club):** Stage plays and street theatre on social themes.
- **Lens (Photography & Film Club):** Photography walks, short-film making and event coverage.

## Sports and Others

- **Aurora Sports Council:** Cricket, football, basketball, badminton and athletics teams.
- **Entrepreneurship Cell (E-Cell):** Mentors student startups, runs pitch events and connects
  students with investors and the AIT incubation center.
- **National Service Scheme (NSS):** Community service, blood donation drives and village outreach.

## Flagship Events

- **Spectra** — the annual techno-cultural festival held every February, featuring hackathons,
  robotics competitions, concerts and guest lectures.
- **CodeStorm** — a 24-hour hackathon organized by the Aurora Coding Club in March.
- **Ignite** — the entrepreneurship summit organized by the E-Cell in September.

## How to Join

Students can join any club during the "Club Fair" held in the first two weeks of the odd
semester, or by contacting the club coordinator listed on the AIT student portal. A student may
join up to four clubs at a time.
