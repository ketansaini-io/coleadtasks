# Aurora Institute of Technology — Rules & Regulations

## Attendance Policy

- A minimum of **75 percent attendance** in every course is mandatory to be eligible for the
  end-semester examination of that course.
- Students between 65 and 75 percent attendance may apply for condonation with a valid medical
  certificate, subject to approval by the Head of Department.
- A student below 65 percent attendance in a course is detained and must repeat that course.

## Examination Rules

- Students must carry their AIT identity card and hall ticket to every examination.
- Use of mobile phones, smart watches or any unauthorized electronic device in the examination
  hall is strictly prohibited and is treated as malpractice.
- The passing mark in every course is **40 percent** of the total marks.
- A student who fails a course may appear for the supplementary examination held before the next
  semester begins.

## Academic Integrity

- Plagiarism, copying in examinations and submitting someone else's work as your own are serious
  offences. A first offence may lead to a zero in that assessment; a repeat offence may lead to
  suspension for one semester.
- All project and assignment submissions are checked for plagiarism.

## Code of Conduct

- Ragging in any form is strictly banned and is a punishable offence that can lead to expulsion.
- Students must maintain discipline and decorum on campus and wear their ID cards at all times.
- Smoking, alcohol and any prohibited substances are banned on campus.

## Hostel Rules

- Hostel gates close at 9:30 PM. Entry after this time requires prior permission from the warden.
- Visitors are allowed only in the common visiting area during visiting hours (4–6 PM).
- Ragging and any form of harassment in the hostel leads to immediate expulsion from the hostel.

## Grievances

- Students may raise academic or administrative grievances through the online grievance portal on
  the AIT student portal, or by contacting the student counsellor.
- The Anti-Ragging Committee and the Internal Complaints Committee handle complaints
  confidentially. The anti-ragging helpline number is displayed on every notice board.

## Leave and Withdrawal

- Medical leave requires a certificate submitted to the department office within 7 days of
  returning.
- A student wishing to withdraw from a program must submit a written application to the Registrar.
  Refund of fees on withdrawal follows the UGC refund norms.
